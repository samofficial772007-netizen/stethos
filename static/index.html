1NOW

2NEXT

Trained baseline

Learn each machine’s unique “normal” sound from real recordings (autoencoder on spectrograms) instead of a fixed baseline.

3LATER

24/7 listening

A sensor streams audio continuously per machine, with live dashboards and instant alerts when the sound drifts.
